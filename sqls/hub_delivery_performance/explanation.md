## Analysis

- Cost per delivery FELL in every hub from Week 1 to Week 2. Most agents are Fixed.So, when volume rises the same cost is spread over more deliveries.
- Lakeview is the most expensive hub in Week 2. It has the fewest deliveries and was only 56% utilised in par capacity.
- Riverside had the most expensive Week 1 and still has the 2nd highest Week 2 cost, despite being busy. Its many holds mean agents spend paid time on attempts that do not become deliveries.
- Greenfield, Hillcrest and Westwood are the cheapest. These are also the busiest hubs.So, they are already using their agents efficiently.
- Westwood's Week 2 cost dropped by about BDT 4,000 partly because its inactive agent left after Week 1.
