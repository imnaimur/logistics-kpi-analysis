1. One-time hiring costs (onboarding, training, equipment) are referenced in the "costs" tab of cost_sheet.xlsx but this data is not in the dataset. The costs above are salary only. One-time costs would increase the total but also strengthen the case against unnecessary hires.

2. Transfers assume agents can be reassigned across hubs within the same city. If cross-hub transfer is infeasible (contractual or logistical barriers), the 3 transfer positions become 3 additional hires, raising the total to 5 at BDT 97,500/month. Even in this worst case, 5 is still fewer than the requested 8.

3. The forecast is based on a trend from 2 weeks of data. If volumes do not materialise, even 2 hires may be excessive. The plan mitigates this by keeping the committed spend small (BDT 39,000/ month) and retaining flexibility.

4. Planning capacity is 34 attempts per agent per day (85% of the 40-attempt standard), 7 days per week = 238 attempts per agent per week. Required agents are rounded UP (CEIL) so no hub is planned above target.

5. All utilisation and gap calculations count only Active agents. Inactive agents left at the end of Week 1 and are excluded from Week 2 and forecast calculations.

6. The average delivery charge is BDT 65 per order (from the orders table), used where revenue impact is referenced. This is the company's direct revenue; it does not include COD handling or other indirect revenue.
